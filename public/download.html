Requirements & Steam testing

Requires .NET Framework 4.8 and Microsoft WebView2 Evergreen, usually already installed on Windows. This portable development build is unsigned.

Steam PvP requires separate signed-in Steam accounts and this Windows version. Quick Match is 1v1; private Steam rooms also support 2v2. Online duel supports browser crossplay without Steam.

For 2v2, Blue starts. Teammates share 40 castles per color. Four consecutive passes finish the battle; all four players must agree to a rematch, then teams swap colors.
